# Powerful Weapons Recipes

This mod adds crafting recipes for [Shinku's Powerful Weapons](https://www.curseforge.com/hytale/mods/shinkus-powerful-weapons)

## Credits & Dependency

All credits go to [NikaidouShinku](https://www.curseforge.com/members/nikaidoushinku/projects), the creator of [Shinku's Powerful Weapons](https://www.curseforge.com/hytale/mods/shinkus-powerful-weapons), who gave me permission to publish this recipes mod.

You will need to first install [Shinku's Powerful Weapons](https://www.curseforge.com/hytale/mods/shinkus-powerful-weapons) in order for my mod to work.

## Recipes

Recipes are obtainable by defeating the Gobliterator.
There is a 15% chance the Gobliterator drops one of the five weapon recipes.

## Crafting

### Arcanist's Workbench

#### Weapons Tab

A new tab has been added to the Arcanist's Workbench for the Powerful weapons.


#### Tier 2 Upgrade

The Arcanist's Workbench now has a Tier 2 upgrade. This upgrade is required in order to craft the weapons.
Crafting the weapons also requires Memories level 3.

Upgrade cost:
- 30 Adamantite Ingot
- 10 Enkindled Sigil
- 30 Essence of Fire
- 50 Essence of Ice
- 200 Essence of the Void
